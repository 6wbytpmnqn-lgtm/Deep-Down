DEEP DOWN: SUBMARINE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Deep Down: Submarine is developed by Niklas Sälle. This policy explains how the game handles information and how to contact the developer about privacy.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your best score, earned coin balance, unlocked and selected submarine skins, and music, sound effects and haptics preferences on your device. If you use Game Center, it also stores your game-specific player identifier and pending or submitted best scores locally to associate scores with the correct account and retry submissions.

This information supports gameplay, saves your progress and enables the leaderboard. It is not sent to a server operated by the developer. Device backups managed by Apple may include this local app data, depending on your settings.

Apple Game Center

You can play without signing in to Game Center. When you are signed in, the game uses Apple's Game Center service and submits eligible completed-run scores to the leaderboard. Apple processes these scores in connection with your Game Center account. Your nickname, avatar and scores may be visible to other players according to Apple's Game Center rules and your account settings.

The game does not receive your Apple Account password or request access to your contacts or Game Center friends list. Apple manages Game Center authentication and its own processing of account and gameplay information. See Game Center & Privacy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising, tracking and device permissions

The current game contains no advertising SDKs, third-party analytics or tracking tools. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. The developer does not sell your personal information or use game data for targeted advertising.

Contacting the developer

If you email the developer, your email address and the information you include will be used to respond to your request and provide support. Please avoid sending passwords or other sensitive information. Correspondence is processed through the developer's email provider and retained only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local progress remains on your device until the app's data is removed. To remove it, delete the app rather than offloading it. Restoring a device backup may restore saved progress. Deleting the app does not delete scores already submitted to Game Center.

You can stop future Game Center score submissions by signing out of Game Center in your device settings. Apple controls retention and deletion of information held by Game Center; use Apple's account and privacy controls or contact Apple for help with that information.

For personal information held by the developer, you may request access, correction or deletion, and exercise other rights available under applicable privacy law, by emailing the address below. The developer cannot directly erase data held independently by Apple. You may also contact your local data protection authority if you have a privacy concern.

Children's privacy

The game does not ask children to provide personal information. Game Center access and sharing for children's accounts are managed by Apple and parental controls. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer by email, please contact the developer to request deletion.

This website

This policy page does not include analytics, advertising, third-party fonts or tracking scripts. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game's features or information handling change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.
